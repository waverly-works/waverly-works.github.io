Product Design · HackAThing 2024

Waddle 🐧

An app that connects students with their peers via easy access to on and off-campus community events — and the ability to create their own.

Role

UI/UX Design & Research

Timeline

48 Hours

Team

Catherine Huang, Waverly Huang, Elaine Zhang, Jenny Zhuang

Tools

Figma, Adobe Suite

01 — Mission

Students are disconnected — even on campus

The HackAThing prompt asked us to solve a real problem for students. We kept landing on the same one: loneliness. Not just social isolation, but the structural kind — events scattered across platforms, off-campus opportunities invisible to most, and no easy way to create your own gathering unless you were already part of a club.

We surveyed 93 students and heard the same frustrations over and over. Waddle is our answer to all of them.

02 — Decisions

Problems → design choices

events = scary

Students are intimidated by bigger events and don't have enough information to decide whether to attend

→
peer review & media gallery

Event detail pages include peer reviews and a photo/video gallery — giving real context before committing to showing up

no club = no involvement

Students don't know how to get involved unless they're already in a club or organization

→
anybody can host

Any student can host — individually or as a group — without needing institutional backing or a pre-existing org

promotion confusion

Events are spread across email, Instagram, physical flyers, and word of mouth — no single place to look

→
information in one place

Waddle consolidates all on and off-campus events into one filterable feed, eliminating the need to cross-reference platforms

lack of motivation

No existing app gives students a reason to keep engaging once the novelty wears off

→
gamified profile badges

A gamified profile with badges and an event archive creates personal stakes and a social record worth maintaining

03 — Solution

Key features

Host an Event

Any student can create a gathering — no club membership required.

Individual or group hosting

Create an event solo to find like-minded peers, or co-host with others to form an unofficial group

Low barrier to entry

Friendly UI keeps the process approachable — making an event shouldn't feel like a bureaucratic task

Find Events

Everything in one place, filtered exactly the way you need it.

Consolidated feed

All active events — on and off campus — in a single view, no platform-hopping required

Tag-based filtering

Students can narrow down by interest or event type without digging through unrelated results

Event Details

Enough information upfront that showing up doesn't feel like a gamble.

Peer reviews

Students can read firsthand accounts from classmates — reducing the intimidation of unfamiliar events

Photo & video gallery

A visual look at what an event is actually like, not just what the host says it is

Profile & Memories

A reason to keep showing up — built into the experience.

Badges & achievements

Earned by hosting events, attending gatherings, leaving reviews, or joining new groups — motivation that scales with engagement

Event archive

A personal record of everything you've attended or hosted — the social history that makes the app worth coming back to

Takeaway

This project showed me how much existing event apps miss by only solving half the problem — they help you find events, but they don't help you feel like you belong. Waddle was about closing that gap. We built it in 48 hours, but the problem it addresses is real: RISD and Brown are a five-minute walk apart, and students from both campuses rarely cross that distance. I'd love to keep pushing this idea further. 🐧
